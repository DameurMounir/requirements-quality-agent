# Draft non-functional requirements

Document ID: `ABS-NFR-001`  
Version: `1.0`  
Classification: `SYNTHETIC-PUBLIC`

[NFR-001] Application pages shall load fast.
[NFR-002] For the synthetic load profile of 200 concurrent sessions, the 95th-percentile server response time for application read requests shall be at most 800 milliseconds during a 30-minute test.
[NFR-003] The portal shall support all browsers.
[NFR-004] All onboarding data shall be secure.
[NFR-005] The service shall provide high availability.
[NFR-006] The portal shall delete application audit events after 30 calendar days.
[NFR-007] The portal shall reject an uploaded file when its detected type is not PDF, JPEG, or PNG, or when its size exceeds 10 MiB, and shall record the rejection reason.
[NFR-008] The solution shall be scalable.
[NFR-009] The portal shall be available and responsive during peak hours.
[NFR-010] Recovery from a service interruption shall happen within an acceptable time.
