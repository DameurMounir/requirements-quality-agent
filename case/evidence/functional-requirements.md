# Draft functional requirements

Document ID: `ABS-FR-001`  
Version: `1.0`  
Classification: `SYNTHETIC-PUBLIC`

[FR-001] The portal shall verify a new applicant quickly.
[FR-002] Send a confirmation to the customer.
[FR-003] When an applicant submits a complete application, the portal shall assign a unique 12-character application ID and display it on the confirmation page.
[FR-004] The portal shall make document upload easy for applicants.
[FR-005] The application form shall be user-friendly.
[FR-006] When an account becomes active, the portal shall email a welcome message to the applicant's registered email address within five minutes.
[FR-007] The portal shall allow an applicant to save a draft after all currently entered fields pass format validation and shall retain that draft for 30 calendar days.
[FR-008] The portal shall activate every standard-risk account automatically after document validation.
[FR-009] The portal shall request additional evidence as necessary for premium customers.
[FR-010] The portal shall store onboarding data.
[FR-011] The portal shall validate identity, create the customer profile, and notify the Sales Manager.
[FR-012] When document validation fails, the portal shall show the failed document name, the validation reason, and a resubmission action without exposing internal rule identifiers.
[FR-013] The Operations dashboard shall be intuitive.
[FR-014] The portal shall upload, crop, compress, and classify each identity document.
[FR-015] The portal shall accept identity evidence from a trusted source.
[FR-016] The system shall send the applicant a welcome email no later than five minutes after activation.
[FR-017] The portal shall give Operations enough information to handle exceptions efficiently.
[FR-018] An Onboarding Specialist shall be able to return an incomplete application with one or more reason codes and an optional message of at most 500 characters.
[FR-019] The portal shall notify the right people when an application changes status.
[FR-020] The search function shall return useful results for Operations staff.
