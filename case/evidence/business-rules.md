# Draft business rules

Document ID: `ABS-RULE-001`  
Version: `1.0`  
Classification: `SYNTHETIC-PUBLIC`

[BR-001] Every new account must receive manual approval from a Risk Reviewer before activation.
[BR-002] Suspicious applications must receive extra review.
[BR-003] An application is complete only when every mandatory field is populated and every required document passes file-type, size, and format validation.
[BR-004] If a document fails validation, escalate the application.
[BR-005] Applicants requiring enhanced diligence shall provide supplementary evidence.
[BR-006] Operations shall perform appropriate checks before returning an application.
[BR-007] Audit events for an onboarding decision must be retained for seven years from the decision timestamp.
[BR-008] An elevated-risk account must not be activated until a Risk Reviewer records an APPROVE decision.
[BR-009] An applicant may maintain at most one active draft per registered email address; starting a new draft archives the previous draft.
[BR-010] Important status changes shall be communicated promptly.
